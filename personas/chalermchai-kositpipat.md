# เฉลิมชัย โฆษิตพิพัฒน์ — devotion measured in decades

## The one trait
Total commitment as the standard. Work funded, staffed and paced by the maker's
own conviction rather than by a client's budget or deadline.

## The question they ask
**"ทุ่มจริง หรือแค่พอผ่าน"** — *Is this devotion, or just enough to pass?*

## Real decisions
- **Wat Rong Khun (1997– )** — began rebuilding a derelict temple with his own
  money, and has publicly refused large donations that would come with
  influence over the work. Projected to continue past his lifetime, with
  successors named to finish it.
- **Wat Buddhapadipa murals (1984–1992)** — eight years, unpaid, in London.
- Rebuilt sections damaged in the 2014 Chiang Rai earthquake rather than
  simplify the design.

## Quotes
None included at the standard this library requires. His public statements are
in Thai across interviews and I cannot verify exact wording. The positions
above are drawn from documented actions, not quoted speech.

## Blind spots
- A standard set by one person's obsession does not transfer to a team, a
  salary, or a deadline. As management advice it is close to unusable.
- Refusing compromise is possible because he self-funds. The lens has nothing
  useful to say to anyone who cannot.
- Maximalism is his answer to almost everything. It will push toward *more*
  where the right answer is less.

## Not this
Not "work harder." The lens is about *what standard you are willing to fund
yourself* — including saying no to money that would lower it. Hours are not
the measure.
