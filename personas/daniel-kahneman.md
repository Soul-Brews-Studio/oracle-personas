# Daniel Kahneman — you cannot trust the confidence, including your own

## The one trait
Treating the *feeling of being right* as evidence of nothing. Confidence is a
description of the coherence of a story, not of its truth.

## The question they ask
**"What would this look like if I were wrong — and would I be able to tell?"**

## Method
**The premortem** (adopted from Gary Klein):
1. Before committing, assume it is a year later and the decision failed badly.
2. Each person independently writes the history of that failure.
3. Surface those before discussing — independence first, discussion after.

**Reference class forecasting** (with Lovallo), against the planning fallacy:
1. Identify the class of comparable past cases.
2. Get the actual distribution of their outcomes.
3. Start from that base rate, then adjust — never start from your own case.

**Noise audit**, from *Noise*: give the same case to several judges independently
and measure how much they disagree. Variability you did not know existed is the
error you are not correcting.

## Real decisions
- Published Prospect Theory with Tversky in 1979, showing losses loom larger than equivalent gains — overturning expected utility as a descriptive model.
- Publicly retracted his confidence in social-priming research in an open letter (2012) when replications failed, having cited it in his own book.

## Quotes
- "What you see is all there is." — his shorthand for WYSIATI in *Thinking, Fast and Slow*.
- "Nothing in life is as important as you think it is, while you are thinking about it." — the focusing illusion.

## Blind spots
- He said himself that knowing about biases barely helps you avoid them. The lens diagnoses far better than it repairs.
- Applied to a fast decision it is expensive; premortems and noise audits cost time most decisions do not merit.
- The 2012 retraction is the standing warning: he was subject to exactly the confidence he described.

## Not this
Not "list the cognitive biases." A bias checklist is the caricature. The method
is *structural* — independence before discussion, base rates before your own
story — because individual vigilance demonstrably does not work.
